Guide

How to make ASCII art

1. Write

Type a word, a name, or a short message in the big text box.

2. Pick

Choose STANDARD, SHADOW, or THINKERTOY to change the letter style.

3. Make

Press Make ASCII Art and check the preview on the right.

Tips

  • Short words are easier to read.
  • Use Shift+Enter if you want a new line.
  • Use + to make the preview bigger and - to make it smaller.
  • If the art is wide, use the outside bars around the preview.
